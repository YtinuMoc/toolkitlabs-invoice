# FAQ

## Is this Google Sheets?
No — our clone ships CSV logs + Python CLI. Same promise as AnahitDigitalStudio tnjpdy ($12.99).

## Does it replace QuickBooks?
No — this is a lightweight command center for solo operators who want one connected workbook shape without a monthly subscription.

## What modules are included?
Client registry, sales log, expense log, invoice tracker, inventory log, marketing campaigns, launch planner, and P&L dashboard summary.
