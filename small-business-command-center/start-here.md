# Start here

1. Copy each `*-template.csv` to your working folder.
2. Fill client registry once — every other tab reads the same clients.
3. Log sales and expenses weekly.
4. Track invoices with due dates — unpaid list totals itself.
5. Run `business_dashboard.py` on your CSVs for the command-center summary.
