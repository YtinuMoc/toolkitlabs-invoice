# 12 copy-paste message templates
Clone of Orion muieqx STR Host AI Toolkit. Replace [BRACKETS] before sending.

## 1. Pre-arrival (3 days out)

Hi [GUEST NAME] — looking forward to hosting you [CHECK-IN DATE]. Check-in is [TIME] at [ADDRESS]. Door code: [CODE] (active from [TIME]). WiFi: [NETWORK] / [PASSWORD]. Parking: [INSTRUCTIONS]. Reply here if you need anything before arrival.

## 2. Day-of check-in

Welcome! Your code [CODE] is live. Inside you'll find [KEY ITEMS: towels, guidebook, etc.]. Trash/recycling: [RULES]. Quiet hours: [TIMES]. I'm a message away if anything's unclear — safe travels today.

## 3. Mid-stay check-in

Hi [GUEST NAME] — hope you're settling in well. Anything missing or not working? A quick reply helps me fix small things before they become big ones. Enjoy [LOCAL TIP].

## 4. Checkout reminder

Morning checkout is [TIME] tomorrow. Please [DISHES/TRASH/KEYS/THERMOSTAT]. No rush on the exact minute — just ping me when you're out. Thanks for staying!

## 5. Review nudge (soft)

Thanks again for staying, [GUEST NAME]. If you have 30 seconds, an honest review helps future guests know what to expect. Either way, you're welcome back anytime.

## 6. Maintenance apology + fix

Hi [GUEST NAME] — sorry about [ISSUE]. I've scheduled [FIX] by [TIME]. In the meantime: [WORKAROUND]. I'll follow up once it's resolved.

## 7. Compensation offer

I want to make this right. For [ISSUE], I'd like to offer [REFUND % / CREDIT / LATE CHECKOUT]. If that works, I'll process it today. Let me know.

## 8. House rules reminder (firm-friendly)

Thanks for flagging this. Just a reminder per listing rules: [RULE]. Happy to help within those guidelines — let me know what you need.

## 9. Extra guest request decline

Thanks for asking. The booking is for [X] guests and the space is set up for that count. I can't accommodate additional overnight guests, but [DAY VISIT POLICY IF ANY].

## 10. Early check-in yes

Good news — the place is ready. You can check in from [TIME] instead of [STANDARD TIME]. Same code: [CODE]. See you soon.

## 11. Early check-in no

I'd love to, but we have a turnover until [TIME]. Check-in stays [STANDARD TIME]. You can drop bags at [LOCATION IF AVAILABLE] or I can recommend [CAFE NEARBY] to wait.

## 12. Post-stay rebooking

Hi [GUEST NAME] — hope [SEASON] treated you well. If you're planning another trip to [CITY], I'd love to host you again. Reply with dates and I'll send a direct offer before public calendar opens.
