# FAQ

**Is this a Notion template?**
No — our clone ships CSV logs + Python CLI. Same promise as AnahitDigitalStudio batvmj ($8.99).

**What metrics matter most?**
Outbound clicks to your product page. Impressions without clicks are vanity.

**How do performance ratings work?**
Weighted score: outbound click rate (50%) + pin click rate (30%) + save rate (20%). Excellent ≥ 3.0 composite.

**Can I use this with Etsy/Gumroad sellers?**
Yes — designed for digital product sellers using Pinterest as a search engine.
