# FAQ — Freelancer Finance Tracker

## Does this work in Google Sheets or only Excel?

Both. Import the CSV templates into Google Sheets (File → Import → Upload) or open in Excel. The CLI reads CSV exports from either.

## Do I need to know spreadsheet formulas?

No. Pale yellow cells in the original workbook are your data; black cells are formulas. Our clone uses CSV + CLI — you type numbers, the dashboard calculates.

## Will this replace my accountant?

No — it bridges now and tax season. Set aside taxes as you earn. Confirm final filings with a tax professional.

## What if I already use QuickBooks or Wave?

This is a faster layer for "how much can I actually spend right now" without opening heavier software.

## Can I use this for more than one tax year?

Duplicate the CSV files at year start, or add a `year` column and filter exports.

## Is my data private?

Yes. Local CSV files or your own Google Sheet — nothing is sent to Toolkit Labs unless you email support.
