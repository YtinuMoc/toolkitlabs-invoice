# FAQ — Gumroad Payout Tracker clone

**Does this connect to Gumroad automatically?**  
No — same as Nagaraj's template. Export payout data from Gumroad Settings → Payouts and enter rows manually. The CLI does the math and summaries.

**What charts does it include?**  
The CLI prints monthly calendar totals, fee rate, and a kanban-style status board (pending / in transit / done). Import the CSVs into Google Sheets or Excel for bar/line/donut charts — same workflow as the original.

**Who is this for?**  
Gumroad creators, affiliate marketers, and small business owners tracking payouts, fees, and commissions in one place.
