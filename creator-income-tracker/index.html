FAQ

Do I need special software?

No. CSV + Python CLI (stdlib). PattyBun's original works free in Google Sheets and opens in Excel.

Other platforms besides Gumroad, Etsy, Shopify, Payhip?

Yes — use platform other and set fee=12.34 in notes.

Auto-sync from Gumroad/Etsy?

No — manual entry. The tracker does fee math (same as PattyBun).

More products or platforms?

Append rows to the revenue log — no CLI row cap.

VAT or international tax?
