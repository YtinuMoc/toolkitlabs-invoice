# Start Here — Monthly Social Content Pack

Clone of Orion lujnvj Start-Here guide.

## What this pack is

A full month of **fill-in-and-post** social captions plus a calendar that tells you what to post when. You add your own food photos — phone by a window is enough.

## Weekly batching routine (60 minutes)

| Step | Time | Do this |
|------|------|---------|
| 1 | 15 min | Shoot 5–8 photos/videos (hero dish, prep, team, space) |
| 2 | 20 min | Open `content-calendar.md` — copy 5 caption numbers for this week |
| 3 | 15 min | Fill `[brackets]` in `captions-all.txt` for each caption |
| 4 | 10 min | Schedule posts in Meta Business Suite or post live |

## Format guide

- **Feed** — square or 4:5 photo + caption
- **Reel** — 15–30 sec clip + short caption or on-screen text
- **Story** — poll, behind-the-scenes, or quote card
