# 30 caption templates — full guide (Orion lujnvj clone)

## Dish spotlights (#1–#5)

**#1 Hero shot:** `[DISH NAME]. That's it. That's the post. 😍 Made fresh with [KEY INGREDIENT] — [reason it's special]. 📍 [neighborhood] · [link]`

**#2 New menu item:** `NEW on the menu: [DISH]. [2 sentences on flavor + who it's for]. Available [days/times]. [link]`

**#3 Limited offer:** `This week only: [OFFER] when you [ACTION]. [Why now]. Link in bio or DM "DEAL".`

**#4 Bundle promo:** `[BUNDLE NAME]: [items] for [price]. Perfect for [occasion]. Order: [link]`

**#5 Signature dish:** `If you've never tried our [SIGNATURE DISH], this is your sign. [One line on what makes it iconic].`

## Behind-the-scenes (#6–#9)

**#6 Prep Reel:** `You asked what happens before [DISH] hits your table. [2-sentence story]. 🎥`

**#7 Kitchen BTS:** `6am in the kitchen: [what you're prepping]. [One line on craft/quality].`

**#8 Team spotlight:** `Meet [NAME] — [role]. [One fun fact]. Next time you're in, say hi!`

**#9 Ingredient spotlight:** `We source [INGREDIENT] from [source/region]. [Why it matters for flavor].`

## Engagement (#10–#16)

**#10 Customer question:** `Quick question: [question about menu/preference]? Comment below — we're reading every reply.`

**#11 Fill-in-blank:** `The best [category] in [city] is __________. (We'll wait.)`

**#12 Seasonal tie-in:** `[Season/holiday] means [dish/drink] season. [One line hook]. Available [dates].`

**#13 UGC repost:** `@[handle] came in for [dish] and we're still thinking about this photo. Tag us — we repost favorites.`

**#14 This or that:** `Settle a debate: [A] or [B]? 🥊 Drop your pick 👇`

**#15 Open question:** `What's your go-to order when you're [mood/occasion]? We'll reply with a recommendation.`

**#16 Hot take:** `Unpopular opinion: [food opinion]. Agree or fight us in the comments.`

## Social proof (#17–#19)

**#17 Milestone:** `[NUMBER] [guests/orders/reviews] and we're still [emotion]. Thank you [city].`

**#18 Review repost:** `"[quote]" — [name] ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Thank you.`

**#19 Regular shoutout:** `To everyone who orders [dish] every [frequency] — you keep us going. 🙏`

## Seasonal & timely (#20–#23)

**#20 Weekend push:** `Weekend plans? [Offer/dish]. [Hours/location]. See you [day].`

**#21 Weather day:** `[Weather] today = [matching dish/drink]. Warm up / cool down with [item].`

**#22 Brunch Saturday:** `Saturday brunch: [items]. [Time window]. No reservations needed / Reserve: [link]`

**#23 Holiday hours:** `[Holiday] hours: [open/closed times]. [Special menu note if any].`

## Brand / team / community (#24–#27)

**#24 Values:** `We believe [value]. That's why [concrete example in your kitchen/service].`

**#25 Origin story:** `We started because [short origin]. [One line on what hasn't changed].`

**#26 Local love:** `Proud to be part of [neighborhood/city]. [Local partnership or community tie-in].`

**#27 Supplier shoutout:** `Shoutout to [local supplier] for [product]. Local ingredients, real flavor.`

## Call-to-action closers (#28–#30)

**#28 Reserve CTA:** `Tables for [day/event] going fast. Reserve: [link] or call [phone].`

**#29 Order CTA:** `Lunch in 20 minutes? Order ahead: [link]. We'll have it ready.`

**#30 Follow CTA:** `New here? Follow for [what you post — weekly specials, BTS, new dishes].`
