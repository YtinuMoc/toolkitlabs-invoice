# Monthly Social Content Pack for Restaurants & Cafés (Orion lujnvj clone)

Shameless clone of [Orion's Monthly Social Content Pack ($99)](https://nexusai82.gumroad.com/l/lujnvj).

## Files

- `start-here.md` — how to use the pack + weekly batching routine
- `caption-templates.md` — 30 fill-in-the-blank captions across 8 categories
- `captions-all.txt` — all 30 captions numbered for copy-paste
- `content-calendar.md` — 4-week posting calendar (5 posts/week)
- `hashtag-sets.md` — 8 cuisine hashtag banks + local template
- `caption-templates-free.md` — 5 free caption templates (dev.to lead magnet)

## Quick start

1. Read `start-here.md` — batch captions once a week.
2. Open `content-calendar.md` — see what to post each day.
3. Copy caption #N from `captions-all.txt`, fill `[brackets]`, add your photo.
4. Paste hashtags from `hashtag-sets.md` (8–15 per post).

Openly AI-assisted content — honestly made, no fake claims.
