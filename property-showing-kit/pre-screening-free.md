# Free pre-screening questions (Orion trsoxh clone)

Use these five questions before scheduling any showing. Copy-paste into email, text, or a listing platform reply.

## 1. Move-in timeline

> What date do you need to move in? (Our unit is available [DATE].)

Filters "just browsing" and mismatched timelines.

## 2. Household + income fit

> How many occupants will live in the unit, and is your combined gross monthly income at least 3× the rent ($[RENT] × 3 = $[MIN])?

Stops showings for applicants who cannot qualify on paper.

## 3. Pets

> Do you have pets? If yes, what type and weight? Our policy: [YOUR PET POLICY].

Avoids surprise pet conflicts at the door.

## 4. Eviction / credit honesty

> Any evictions or broken leases in the last 3 years? (Honest answers save us both time.)

## 5. Showing commitment

> Can you attend a 15-minute showing at [ADDRESS] on [DATE/TIME OPTIONS]? Reply with your top two slots.
