# Follow-Up Templates

Clone of Orion trsoxh module 4 — send within 2 hours of showing.

## 1. Apply now (hot lead)

**Subject:** Application link — [ADDRESS]

> Hi [NAME],
>
> Great meeting you at [ADDRESS] today. The unit is still available for [MOVE-IN DATE].
>
> Apply here: [LINK]. First complete qualified application gets priority.
>
> Reply if you have questions. I'll confirm receipt within 24 hours.
>
> — [YOUR NAME]

## 2. Nurture (still deciding)

**Subject:** Any questions on [ADDRESS]?

> Hi [NAME], thanks for touring [ADDRESS] today. I know choosing a place takes time.
>
> Happy to answer questions about [pets/parking/neighbors/lease terms]. The unit is holding until [DATE] — I'll update you if that changes.
>
> — [YOUR NAME]

## 3. Not a fit (polite close)

**Subject:** Re: [ADDRESS]

> Hi [NAME], thanks for your time at today's showing. Based on [timeline/income/pets], this unit may not match what you need.
>
> I don't have a better match right now, but I'll keep your contact if something similar opens up.
>
> — [YOUR NAME]

## 4. Competing offer response

**Subject:** Update on [ADDRESS]

> Hi [NAME], heads up — we received another application for [ADDRESS]. You're still welcome to apply before [DEADLINE TIME] if you're interested.
>
> Apply: [LINK]. No pressure either way — just wanted transparency.
>
> — [YOUR NAME]
