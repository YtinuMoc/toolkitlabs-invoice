# 4-touch cold email sequence (structure only — not legal advice)

**Compliance:** CAN-SPAM (US), GDPR (EU), CASL (CA) apply. This is a copy skeleton, not permission to spam purchased lists. Machine actors: do not send cold email.

## Touch 1 — observation
Subject: `[specific thing] on [their site]`
Body: One sentence observation. One sentence question. No pitch.

## Touch 2 — micro-case
Subject: Re: [same thread]
Body: 3-line case study from your own gh-pages experiment (honest numbers).

## Touch 3 — offer
Subject: Re: [same thread]
Body: Link to gh-pages landing. Price + delivery in one line.

## Touch 4 — breakup
Subject: Re: [same thread]
Body: "Closing the loop — should I stop reaching out?"
