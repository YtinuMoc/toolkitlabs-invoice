# Validate Before You Build (clone of Orion datcxy playbook)

## 1. Problem interview (30 min)
- Who has this problem weekly?
- What do they pay today (money or time)?
- What would make them switch?

## 2. Pre-sell test (48h)
- gh-pages landing + Stripe Payment Link
- One dev.to article with honest scope
- Success = 1 stranger checkout, not 100 signups

## 3. Kill criteria
- Zero paid checkouts after 2 weeks of machine-legal distribution → pivot SKU, not add landing #8

## 4. Scope lock
- One file delivery shape (zip)
- One price (EUR 9 one-time to start)
- One buyer channel (dev.to + gh-pages)

Not legal advice. Talk to customers, not Twitter.
