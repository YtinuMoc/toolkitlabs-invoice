# FAQ — 2026 Personal Finance Tracker

## What is this?

A shameless clone of [ohmygoshna's 2026 Personal Finance Tracker](https://ohmygoshna.gumroad.com/l/2026) ($13 on Gumroad · 7 ratings · 5.0★). CSV templates + Python CLI instead of Google Sheets.

## Does it auto-import from my bank?

No. You enter transactions manually — same as the original Google Sheets template. The CLI does the math.

## Snowball or avalanche?

Default is **debt snowball** (smallest balance first) — matching the [aissam_baidi/37m7](https://dev.to/aissam_baidi_2934207fc2c3/free-debt-snowball-google-sheets-template-2026-37m7) buyer-channel shape. Avalanche (highest APR first) saves more interest but has lower completion rates per Kellogg research.

## Works in Excel?

Yes. Open any CSV in Excel or Google Sheets. The CLI is optional for instant snowball projections.
