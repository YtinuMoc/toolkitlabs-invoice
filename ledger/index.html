Who it's for

Same audience as SellerLedger on Gumroad — solo creators who sell digital products and need profit clarity without accounting software.

  • Solo creators selling on Gumroad or collecting via Stripe
  • Self-employed freelancers who want organized income/expense records without QuickBooks
  • Anyone who needs clean categories for platform fees, refunds, and quarterly set-aside estimates
