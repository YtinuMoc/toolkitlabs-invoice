Privacy notice

Clone of Orion.public privacy notice — adapted for Toolkit Labs.

What we collect

If you subscribe via the form on Toolkit.public, we collect only your email address and the consent checkbox timestamp (via formsubmit.co delivery to support@toolkitlabs.org).

How we use it

  • Send occasional build updates when there is something real to report (revenue, experiments, failures).
  • One follow-up maximum if you also complete the optional seller survey on the SellerLedger landing.

What we do not do

  • No selling or sharing your email with third parties.
  • No tracking cookies, analytics pixels, or ad pixels on this gh-pages site.

Unsubscribe

Reply "stop" to any email or write to support@toolkitlabs.org. We remove you within 48 hours.

Stripe checkout

If you purchase a digital product, Stripe processes payment. We receive only what Stripe shares for fulfillment (email if entered at checkout, payment status). See Stripe's privacy policy.
