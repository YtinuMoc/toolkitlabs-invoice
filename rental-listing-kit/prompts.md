## Property types (6–12)
6. **Studio / efficiency** — "Write a 250-word listing for a [SQ FT] studio in [CITY]. Emphasize layout efficiency, storage, and who it's perfect for (single professional). Include title + 8 bullets."
7. **Luxury unit** — "Write upscale but not pretentious copy for [BR]BR with [HIGH-END AMENITIES]. 300 words. Avoid clichés ('stunning', 'gorgeous')."
8. **Budget / value** — "Write honest value-focused listing for [BR]BR at $[RENT] in [AREA]. Lead with price + what's included. No overselling."
9. **Duplex / house** — "Write listing for entire [BR]BR house (not apartment). Mention yard, driveway, privacy vs apartment living. 300 words."
10. **Room rental** — "Write shared-housing listing for private room in [BR]BR house. Cover shared spaces, housemate vibe, quiet hours, lease terms. 200 words."
11. **Furnished short-term** — "30–90 day furnished rental. Mention what's included (linens, kitchenware, WiFi speed). 250 words."
12. **Senior-friendly** — "Ground floor / elevator / grab bars / walk-in shower. Write listing emphasizing accessibility without clinical tone. 250 words."

## Market situations (13–20)
13. **High turnover market** — "Unit available immediately in competitive [CITY] market. Write urgency without desperation. CTA for same-week showings."
14. **Slow market** — "Unit vacant 3+ weeks. Rewrite listing to refresh search ranking — new title angle, emphasize one underused perk."
15. **Rent reduction** — "Lowering rent from $[OLD] to $[NEW]. Write transparent update post for Facebook + Zillow refresh. Honest about price change."
16. **Seasonal college town** — "Available [MONTH] near [UNIVERSITY]. Target students or faculty. Mention lease dates, parking for move-in weekend."
17. **Military / base town** — "Near [BASE]. Mention BAH-friendly framing, lease flexibility, pet policy for PCS moves."
18. **New renovation** — "Recently updated: [LIST UPDATES]. Write 'just renovated' angle without before/after you don't have."
19. **Historic / character** — "Older building with [CHARACTER FEATURES]. Balance charm with honest age (no central AC, etc.)."
20. **New construction** — "Built [YEAR], first tenant. Emphasize warranties, modern efficiency, builder-grade finishes."

## Platform-specific (21–28)
21. **Zillow amenity bullets** — "10 Zillow amenity bullets from brief. Start with filters renters search: pets, parking, laundry, AC."
22. **Apartments.com shorten** — "Cut this 300-word listing to 150 words for Apartments.com. Keep pet + parking in first sentence: [PASTE]"
23. **Facebook casual** — "Rewrite for FB Marketplace — conversational, emoji sparingly, 120 words max: [PASTE]"
24. **Craigslist plain** — "Plain-text Craigslist, no HTML, 100 words, phone/email CTA: [PASTE]"
25. **Nextdoor post** — "Neighbor-friendly Nextdoor rental post, 150 words, emphasize quiet tenants + neighborhood fit."
26. **Instagram caption** — "3 Instagram carousel captions for rental photos. Hook in line 1. Link in bio CTA."
27. **Email to waitlist** — "Email past inquiries: unit back on market, what's new, showing slots this week. 120 words."
28. **SMS inquiry reply** — "Prospect texted: [PASTE]. Reply under 160 chars with showing times + one hook."

## Photo + showing copy (29–34)
29. **Photo captions** — "Write 1-line caption per photo filename for upload order: [LIST FILES]"
30. **Virtual tour script** — "60-second self-guided tour voiceover script walking through [ROOM LIST]."
31. **Showing confirmation** — "Confirm showing [DATE/TIME] at [ADDRESS]. Parking instructions, what to bring, ID policy. 80 words."
32. **Post-showing follow-up** — "Prospect toured today, seemed interested. Follow-up email with application link, no pressure. 100 words."
33. **Application instructions** — "Cover letter prompt for tenant: what to include in application email to landlord."
34. **Decline politely** — "Applicant not selected. Polite decline email preserving relationship for future units. 60 words."

## Compliance + honesty (35–40)
35. **Fair housing check** — "Review this listing for fair-housing risk phrases. Flag and suggest neutral rewrites: [PASTE]"
36. **Lead paint / disclosure** — "Pre-1978 building. Add disclosure paragraph that satisfies without scaring off qualified tenants."
37. **HOA rules summary** — "HOA restrictions: [PASTE]. Summarize for listing in 3 bullets tenants care about."
38. **Utility split clarity** — "Tenant pays [UTILITIES]. Write clear utility section — no surprises at lease signing."
39. **Move-in cost breakdown** — "Rent $[X], deposit $[Y], fees $[Z]. Write transparent move-in cost paragraph."
40. **Lease term options** — "Offer 6 / 12 / month-to-month. Write options paragraph with price difference if any."
