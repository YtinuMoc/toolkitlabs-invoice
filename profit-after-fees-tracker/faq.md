# FAQ

**Is this accounting software?** No. Planning tool only — confirm tax rates with a professional.

**Does it connect to Etsy?** No. Enter sales manually or import CSV exports. The CLI does the fee math.

**Why ads ROI?** Etsy's dashboard shows ROAS on revenue. [jartan/207j](https://dev.to/jartan/etsys-ad-dashboard-told-me-i-was-winning-my-bank-account-disagreed-207j) proved sellers need net profit after ads — we clone that buyer channel.
